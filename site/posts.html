How to use this page

  1. Drafts live above. If one needs changes, choose Ask Brad to revise on that exact draft and tell Brad what you want changed. Use "Copy post" only after you approve the final copy.
  2. After posting, tell Claude: "I posted the Monday launch — here's the link: [url]". Brad moves it to "Awaiting metrics".
  3. 24-48h later, open LinkedIn/X analytics, copy the numbers, paste them to Claude: "grade the Monday post — 1,247 impressions, 38 likes, 4 comments, 1 share, 23 link clicks, 2 trial signups".
  4. The post-performance-grader subagent grades A/B/C/D vs benchmarks, writes notes + recommendations, updates posts.json. Refresh this page to see the result.
