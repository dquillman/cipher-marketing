Blind spots, risks and assumptions

  1. Timeline. SEO is a 6–12 month channel. Your measured position is 1 activated user with paid capped at $2/day. Nothing in this plan moves activation this quarter, and treating it as if it will is the fastest way to abandon it at month four — which is exactly when it starts working.
  2. The free-questions decision is a product decision, not a marketing one. Every SERP checked is won by pages giving questions away. Doing this means putting real questions with real reasoning outside the paywall. If you are not willing to, say so now and cut Wave 2 — but then accept that top-of-funnel search is closed to you and the channel is buyer-intent pages only.
  3. Links are the binding constraint, not on-page work. Two of your four target SERPs are owned by affiliate roundups. You can execute every fix in this report perfectly and still not rank, because the winning pages have domain authority you do not have. Budget real effort for placement in those roundups.
  4. EEAT is your exposed flank. Solo founder, AI-assisted content, in a category where a competitor is explicitly attacking AI-written material. A named credentialed reviewer is the highest-value hire-or-favour on this list, and it is not a marketing task.
  5. Every priority here is a hypothesis until Search Console is connected. If one page is already ranking at position 11 for something valuable, that page deserves everything and this list is wrong. Twenty minutes of setup buys the right to be confident. Do it first.
