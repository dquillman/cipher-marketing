Post Scheduler

All times shown in Mountain Time (MT). The workflow: prep posts the night before using LinkedIn's and X's native schedulers — no early mornings needed.

1
Attach video as MEDIA — NOT as text in the body:
a. Click "Copy video path" on the card b. In LinkedIn/X composer, click the camera / "Add photo or video" button c. A file picker window opens — paste the path into its address bar (top of the picker) and hit Enter d. Video attaches as a playable preview. Never paste the path into the post body.
2
Paste the post text into the composer body (where you type the caption). Set the scheduler to your preferred MT time.
3
After it posts — click "Mark as Posted" on the card, paste the live post URL into the field, and click Mark Posted. The dashboard updates instantly.
