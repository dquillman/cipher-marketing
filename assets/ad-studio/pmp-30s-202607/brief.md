# Ad Studio Brief — PMP 30s spot (2026-07)

Campaign slug: `pmp-30s-202607` · Format: 16:9 · Length: **15s** (cut down from 30s, Dave 2026-07-11) · Target LP: cipherexam.com/lp/pmp

## 1. Brand in one line

CipherExam is AI-powered certification prep that teaches you how the exam *thinks* — reasoning, not memorization.

## 2. What they really sell

Not a question bank. The buyer walks into the exam room knowing *why* right answers are right — "the exam stops feeling like a trick." The mechanism is the **Exam Lens** (umbrella marketing term): a per-exam metacognitive framework behind every explanation, plus Bloom's-level classified questions and thinking-traps detection. For PMP specifically: a judgment test, not a knowledge test — 180 questions / 230 minutes of "what would PMI want you to do?"

## 3. Who buys

A working professional (~30s–40s) with real project experience who's been told to "just memorize the process groups" and can feel it isn't working. They've paid (or are about to pay) hundreds of dollars to sit the exam, they study at night after work, and their core fear is not lack of knowledge — it's picking B when the answer is C *and not understanding why*. Global, multi-ethnic, gender-balanced audience — don't cast one persona repeatedly.

## 4. Design DNA

- Background world: very dark slate `#020617`; soft glowing card shapes; dark-purple-and-blue UI abstraction
- Type (post-production only): Satoshi (400/500/700/900) + General Sans — never Inter
- Tone: cinematic, photoreal, restrained, B2B-professional; shallow depth of field; no hype energy
- Voice: plain-spoken, slightly weary-of-bad-prep-tools; approved frames: "Learn how exams think," "Reasoning, not memorization," "The exam stops feeling like a trick"

## 5. Hard avoid-list

- **No first-person exam-taking claims** — Dave hasn't taken these exams
- **No "guaranteed pass"**, no "crush/dominate/smash," no "game-changer/revolutionary/unleash"
- **No fake urgency/scarcity** — no caps, no lifetime deal exist
- **No competitor names** in creative
- **No AI-rendered on-screen text, prices, logos, or numbers** — AI video models hallucinate text (locked lesson from the Veo playbook: `$CipherExam$`, "PMP cones"). ALL text, prices, brand chrome, and captions are added in post. Laptop screens in AI shots = abstract glowing dark-purple/blue card shapes only, never readable UI.
- **Markus Kopko testimonial on PMI surfaces:** institutional credential only ("PMI AI Standards Core Team Member"), name removed — if used at all in this spot
- Offer terms are exactly: free 7-day trial, no credit card required, cancel anytime — don't embellish

## Stage 5 — Story

**Setup:** Night. A project manager in her late 30s studies at the kitchen table, the house dark around her. She's competent — and stuck. **Build:** A practice question narrows to two answers. She deliberates, commits — and it's wrong. Not because she didn't know the material; because she didn't know how PMI thinks. **Shift:** The Exam Lens moment — a calm panel of reasoning unfolds beside the question. Her face changes from frustration to recognition: *that's* what they were asking. **Resolution:** Same table, later. A question appears; she reads it, answers without hesitating, settles back. The exam has stopped feeling like a trick.
