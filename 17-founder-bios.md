## Usage notes

- **Set the bios before posting the first breakdown.** They're the credibility/context the breakdowns lean on.
- **Reddit:** no links in post bodies; let the bio + comment value carry it. Most cert subs ban overt self-promo — earn reputation first.
- **Keep the positioning line identical across surfaces** so it compounds into one recognizable idea ("how the exam thinks").
- **One channel is the spine: LinkedIn** (the chosen Distribution-layer channel). X + Reddit are reinforcement.
