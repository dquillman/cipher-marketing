### What separates the top from the bottom

The status-cost mechanic recorded on 2026-08-21 still holds, but the top of the hashtag
has changed hands. **It is now job supply, not exam content.** Eight of the nineteen
external posts are hiring or job-referral content, and the biggest room in the sample is a
job listing. No exam-pass announcement surfaced at all this month; last month's #1 was one.

Inside that, the separator is sharper than "status cost":

- A post that **asks the reader for something they already have** buys comments.
  Diedra Grant took **60 comments off 49 reactions** by asking strangers for study advice.
  Ours took **19 off 4** by asking for a reason. Nothing else in the sample cleared 8.
- A post that **tests the reader** buys nothing, and a post that **teaches or sells prep**
  buys nothing either: Scholaracad 2, Darron Clark 1, Snehashis 3, Lloye Warner 1.

Giving advice is status-positive. Being tested is status-negative. Our gate survives that
because it reads as an invitation to demonstrate judgment, not as a quiz.
