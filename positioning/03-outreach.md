# Outreach — Booking 5 Real PMP Candidates

**Goal:** 5 booked 20-min calls with people sitting the PMP in the next ~1–3 months. Over-ask — DM ~15–20 to land 5. Aim to fill the calendar in 48 hours.

**Where they are:**
- **r/pmp** (most concentrated — but read rules; no link-dropping, no "DM me to sell"). Peaks 9–11am ET.
- **LinkedIn** — search "studying for PMP", "PMP exam prep", "PMP aspirant", recent #PMP posters.
- **Your own list** — anyone who hit the PMP LP, any beta tester prepping for PMP, anyone who emailed.
- PMP study Discords / Facebook groups if you're already a member (don't cold-join to solicit).

**Incentive:** offer a $10–15 gift card or a free month of Pro for 20 minutes. It dramatically lifts booking rate and is cheap vs. the value of the signal. (Disclose it — never disguise paid research as organic praise.)

---

## DO NOT
- Don't pitch in the DM. You're asking for *their opinion*, not their money. The moment it smells like a sale, reply rate craters.
- Don't post a link to CipherExam in r/pmp threads (AutoMod + community will nuke it). Value/question first; the tool comes up only on the call.
- Don't only talk to people who'll be nice (friends, fellow founders). They give false positives. You want real candidates with real money and a real exam date.

## Templates

### A) Reddit DM (after they post something prep-related in r/pmp)
> Hey — saw your post about prepping for the PMP. I'm not selling anything; I'm building a PMP study tool and I'm trying to figure out if I'm even describing it right. Could I grab 20 min to hear how you're studying and what's driving you nuts about it? Happy to send a $15 gift card for your time. Either way, good luck on the exam.

### B) LinkedIn connect note (≤300 chars)
> Hi [name] — saw you're prepping for the PMP. I'm building a study tool and need 20 min with real candidates to make sure my pitch isn't nonsense. Not a sales call — your honest take. $15 gift card for your time if you're open to it?

### C) LinkedIn message (already connected)
> Hey [name] — quick one. You're prepping for the PMP, right? I'm building a prep tool and I'm trying to nail how to describe it. Would you give me 20 min of brutally honest feedback this week? Not selling — genuinely want to know if it's confusing or useful. $15 gift card or a free month, your pick. What day works?

### D) Email (your own list / past LP visitors)
> Subject: 20 min + $15 gift card — honest PMP feedback?
>
> Hi [name],
>
> You checked out CipherExam a little while back. I'm not writing to sell — I'm trying to figure out if I'm describing it the right way to people actually sitting the PMP.
>
> Could I grab 20 minutes this week to hear how you're prepping and get your honest reaction? $15 gift card (or a free month of Pro) for your time.
>
> Pick any slot here: [Calendly link]
>
> Thanks either way — and good luck on the exam.
> Dave, founder of CipherExam

### E) Reddit value-comment (warms a thread; never leads with the product)
Reply helpfully to a "how do I study for PMP" thread with one genuinely useful tip (e.g., the difference between recall questions and judgment/scenario questions, and why practice that only gives you the answer doesn't build the second). Then, *only if it fits and rules allow*, soft-mention you're building something in the space and DM a few people who engage. The comment earns the DM.
