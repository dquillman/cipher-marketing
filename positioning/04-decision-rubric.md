# Decision Rubric — Read After All 5 Calls

The point of the test is a **decision**, not a vibe. Tally the three signals across all 5 calls, then follow the table. Don't average feelings — count.

---

## Step 1 — Tally the three signals (from `05-call-log.md`)

| Signal | Count (out of 5) |
|---|---|
| **Clarity** — got it in ~10 sec, played it back right | ___ / 5 |
| **Pull** — asked "how much / how do I get it" before you said price | ___ / 5 |
| **Price expectation ≥ $30/mo** — their unprompted guess put us on the "prep course" shelf, not the "app" shelf | ___ / 5 |
| **Objection = relevance** ("just another question bank") | ___ / 5 |
| **Objection = price** ("$19 is too much") | ___ / 5 |
| **Would start the trial this week** (weight lightly — intent ≠ action) | ___ / 5 |

---

## Step 2 — Read the verdict

### ✅ KEEP THE FRAME — and ship it
**If:** Clarity ≥ 4/5 **and** Pull ≥ 3/5 **and** price-expectation ≥ $30 on ≥ 3/5.
**Means:** the frame put you on the right shelf and the $19 offer pulls. The bottleneck was never the product — it was that the site/ads weren't saying *this*.
**Do next (this is now positioning work, not feature work):**
1. Rewrite the **homepage H1** and the **PMP LP above-the-fold** in the winning frame's language — using the candidates' verbatim phrases.
2. Rewrite the **3 PMP ad hooks** to the same frame.
3. Re-run the next batch of trial traffic against the new copy. Watch activation rate (target >30%).
4. Only then consider features — and only ones the calls said were missing.

### 🔁 PIVOT THE FRAME — same product, new shelf
**If:** Clarity is mixed/low (≤ 3/5) **or** price-expectation came in low (< $30 on most) **or** objection = relevance on ≥ 3/5.
**Means:** they understood the *words* but shelved you next to free apps / question banks → no willingness to pay. The product is fine; the category story is wrong.
**Do next:**
1. Look at *which* frame (A/B/C) scored best on clarity + price-expectation. Lead with that one.
2. If none worked, build a 4th frame from their language — specifically, from how they described the **problem** and the **alternative they'd use instead** (call Q "if this didn't exist…"). Buyers name their own category better than we do.
3. Re-test the new frame on 3 more calls before touching the site. Cheap loop.

### 🛠️ PIVOT THE OFFER — frame's fine, deal isn't compelling
**If:** Clarity ≥ 4/5 **and** they get the shelf (price-expectation ≥ $30) **but** objection = price on ≥ 3/5, **or** Pull ≤ 1/5 despite understanding it, **or** the recurring answer to "what would you need to see to believe it works?" is something we don't offer.
**Means:** they get it and shelve it right, but the deal isn't irrational-to-refuse yet. Usually it's the **Perceived Likelihood** lever (our known-weakest one).
**Do next — turn the Hormozi levers, in this order (cheapest first):**
1. **Likelihood:** add the proof they asked for. Most likely: a readiness/score-prediction signal, more credentialed testimonials, or a visible "here's what you got wrong and why" artifact. (Whatever the call quotes demanded — don't guess.)
2. **Risk reversal:** consider a stronger trial framing (longer trial, "see your weak spots free before you pay") — *not* a fake pass guarantee.
3. **Time-to-value:** make the first-session "aha" (weak-spot reveal) faster and more visible.
4. Re-test the sharpened offer on 3 calls.

### 🚨 GO BACK FURTHER — wrong audience or wrong problem
**If:** ≥ 3/5 said they'd just use free stuff / "nothing", or the frustration they described isn't one CipherExam solves, or you couldn't even book 5 real PMP candidates who cared.
**Means:** the issue is upstream of offer *and* frame. Either these aren't the buyers, or the problem isn't painful enough to pay for.
**Do next:** before any copy or feature work — reconsider segment (is it first-timers? re-sitters? corporate-sponsored?) and whether PMP is even the right beachhead vs. another cert where the pain (and price anchor) is higher.

---

## Step 3 — Write the one decision down
After scoring, write ONE sentence in `05-call-log.md` under "VERDICT":
> "After 5 calls: [KEEP / PIVOT FRAME / PIVOT OFFER / GO BACK]. The winning frame is ___. The exact line a candidate gave me that becomes the new H1 is: '___'. Next concrete step: ___."

That sentence is the entire payoff of the week. It converts "~0 users" from a mystery into a move.
