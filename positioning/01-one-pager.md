## PART 1 — THE CATEGORY FRAME (Dunford)

> The single most important decision: **what shelf does the buyer put us on?** That decision sets the price they expect before they ever see ours.

### The pricing-anchor problem, made concrete

| If they shelve us as… | They compare us to… | Expected price | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| "AI flashcard app" | Quizlet, Anki, free decks | ~$0/mo | $19 feels *expensive* |
| "Another question bank" | Pocket Prep, Examzify | ~$10–15/mo | $19 feels *average* |
| **"AI cert tutor that finds your weak spots before the exam does"** | PMP boot camps, Kaplan, instructor courses | **$50–$400+** | **$19 feels like a steal** |

Same product. The frame moves willingness-to-pay by 10–40×. Right now we may be getting shelved as a question bank — which is why $19 doesn't pull and ~0 convert.

### Three frame candidates to test (pick one to lead, or A/B across the 5 calls)

**Frame A — The diagnostic tutor** *(recommended lead)*
> "CipherExam is an AI tutor that finds the exact weak spots that'll fail you on the PMP — before the exam does — and teaches you the reasoning, not the answer."
- *Competes with:* PMP prep courses / tutors. *Anchor:* $400 boot camp, $575 sit fee.
- *Why it might win:* "finds your weak spots before the exam does" is a fear + outcome in one line. Maps to our real **Readiness Report + thinking-traps detection.**

**Frame B — The judgment trainer**
> "The PMP isn't a knowledge test. It's a judgment test. CipherExam trains the way PMI wants you to think — so the questions stop feeling like traps."
- *Competes with:* scenario-based prep, boot camps. *Anchor:* the exam itself.
- *Why it might win:* matches the real exam pain (scenario questions) and our **Exam Lens** IP. Risk: more abstract — may not trigger "how much?" as fast.

**Frame C — The explanation engine**
> "Every other prep tool tells you the right answer. CipherExam tells you *why* — the reasoning behind every question, so you can solve the ones you've never seen."
- *Competes with:* question banks (Pocket Prep, Examzify) — but reframes *up* by attacking their core weakness (no explanations).
- *Why it might win:* clearest contrast with what they've already tried. Risk: still anchors near question-bank pricing.

> **Test instruction:** Open each call with ONE frame (rotate if you want signal on which lands). The winner is the one where they (a) get it in 10 sec and (b) ask "how much?" unprompted.

## PART 2 — THE GRAND SLAM OFFER (Hormozi)

Four levers. The job: crank Dream + Likelihood **up**, crank Time + Effort **down** — using only things that are *true today*.

### Dream Outcome (what they actually want) — ↑
Not "study more." They want: **pass the PMP on the first try, without burning 3 months and $400 on a boot camp that just makes them memorize.** First-try pass = no $575 re-sit, no telling their manager they failed.

### Perceived Likelihood of Achievement — ↑ *(this is our weakest lever today — listen hard here)*
What we can honestly say right now:
- Every question is **classified by Bloom's level** — you practice the *kind* of thinking the exam tests, not just recall.
- **Exam Lens** teaches the reasoning the way PMI frames decisions — with a reference (PMBOK clause) on every explanation.
- **Thinking-traps detection** tracks the patterns where *you specifically* keep slipping, and a **Readiness Report** shows when you're actually ready.
- Credibility witness: a **PMI AI Standards Core Team member** endorsed the reasoning-framework approach during beta.
- ❌ What we do NOT say: "guaranteed pass," a made-up pass-rate %, or anything we can't back. (Competitors claim 99.6% with no methodology — our move is *transparent reasoning*, not an unverifiable badge.)
- → **Open question for the calls:** what proof would *they* need to believe it works? (This is the highest-value thing you'll learn.)

### Time Delay (how fast they feel value) — ↓
- **First session shows you something:** answer a few questions, see your weak domains and the reasoning immediately.
- **7-day free trial, no credit card** — value before any commitment.
- Free tier: **20 questions/day for the first 7 days** — a real taste, not a 5-question tease.

### Effort & Sacrifice (how hard / how much it costs) — ↓
- **$19/month.** Or $189/year (saves 17%). Cancel anytime.
- vs. a **$400+ boot camp** or **$575 to re-sit** the exam.
- No credit card to start. No long course to sit through — practice in the gaps of your day.
- The tool does the diagnosis: it finds your weak spots *for* you. You don't have to guess what to study.

### The offer in one breath (read this to the prospect)
> "$19 a month — less than the cost of re-sitting the PMP once — for an AI tutor that finds the exact weak spots that'll fail you, teaches you the reasoning PMI tests for, and tells you when you're actually ready. Free for 7 days, no credit card. Cancel anytime."
